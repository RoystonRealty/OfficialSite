These are the micro-markets we transact in most and know best. We also take on select mandates elsewhere in Bengaluru and across Karnataka when the brief calls for it.

Central & South

  • Sadashivanagar

    One of the city's oldest and quietest addresses: large plots, bungalows and established families near Sankey Tank.

  • Richmond Town

    Leafy, central lanes a few minutes from MG Road and the business district.

  • Cooke Town

    Quiet streets of older bungalows and low-rise homes beside Frazer Town.

  • Indiranagar

    100 Feet Road and 12th Main, on the metro, with independent houses and boutique low-rise apartments.

  • Koramangala

    The startup district, with a mix of apartments, independent homes and offices across its blocks.

  • Jayanagar

    Planned, tree-lined and settled. A long-standing choice for families who want to stay put.

  • JP Nagar

    Residential and well connected, close to Bannerghatta Road and the metro.

East

  • Marathahalli

    The Outer Ring Road junction, with steady rental demand from nearby tech parks.

  • Brookefield

    Apartments close to ITPL, popular with working professionals.

  • Whitefield

    Tech parks, international schools and gated communities, now on the metro.

  • Varthur

    A newer belt of villa communities and larger homes.

  • Bellandur

    Apartments along the Outer Ring Road tech corridor.

  • Sarjapur Road

    Gated communities, villas and schools, with strong demand from families.

North

  • Hebbal

    The lakefront gateway to the airport road, close to Manyata Tech Park.

  • Thanisandra

    Newer apartment projects a short drive from Manyata.

  • Jakkur

    Lake views, the aerodrome and a growing number of villa projects.

  • Yelahanka

    Spacious and calmer, with independent homes and planned layouts.

  • Devanahalli

    Plotted developments, land and long-horizon investments.
